# Stateful

Stateful is property accounting for landlords who keep rentals in LLCs. This plugin connects Claude to one workspace at https://withstateful.com/mcp over remote HTTP.

Sign in with your Stateful account, choose the workspace, and ask for profit and loss, a balance sheet, outstanding rent, or a K-1 worksheet. A change is shown as a preview first. Claude saves it only after you confirm. The connector cannot delete posted history or move money.

Setup and permissions: https://withstateful.com/connectors/claude
Support: support@withstateful.com
